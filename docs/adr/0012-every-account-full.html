#12. When every account is full, be on the one that frees up first

#Context

ADR 6 left the case where every account is at its limit alone: the watcher said when the first one resets and stayed on the active account. But the accounts don't reset together. Staying on one that resets at 1:30 AM while another frees up at 10:30 PM means the next claude message fails for three hours longer than it has to, or waits for someone to notice and switch by hand.

#Decision

When the active account is at switch_at, auto is on and no other account has room, the watcher switches to the unpaused account whose limit resets first, if that is at least a minute sooner than the active one's:

  • An account frees up when every window at its limit has reset: a full 5-hour window that resets soon doesn't help while its week is full.
  • Last known numbers count (they are lower bounds until their window resets); an account whose reset time is unknown, or whose usage can't be read at all, is never picked; one with a known reset beats an active one without.
  • The minute's margin keeps two accounts resetting together (the API's reset times jitter) from swapping back and forth.
  • One notification says everything (switched, when it frees up, when the old one does); the "reached its limit" one isn't sent again for the new account.
  • The watcher then looks again a few seconds after the first reset, instead of up to check_every later.

Paused accounts stay out, as everywhere else. With auto off the watcher only says which account frees up first.

#Consequences

  • With every account full, Claude works again at the earliest moment any of them allows, with no one watching the clock.
  • A switch to a full account still changes every running session's account; that's harmless, since none of them could send anything on the old one.
  • An account a session never uses has its numbers asked at most every ten minutes, so its reset time can be a little old; usage only grows inside a window, so the reset time it gives is still the right one.
